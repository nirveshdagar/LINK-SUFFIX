1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Godrej Properties Limited ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you visit the Godrej Verano project website located at Sector 63A, Golf Course Road, Gurgaon ("Site", "Service").

By accessing or using our Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Policy, please do not access the Site.

Plain summary: We collect only the information you voluntarily share (name, phone, email) when you enquire about a unit, and we use it solely to contact you about the project. We never sell your data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out an enquiry form, request a callback, schedule a site visit, or download a brochure. This may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile / telephone number
  • City of residence
  • Preferred unit type (3 / 4 / 5 BHK)
  • Any additional message or preference you share

We also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit the Site, including IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages viewed. This data is aggregated and used for analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide information about Godrej Verano units, pricing, and availability.
  • To schedule and confirm site visits to the project.
  • To send you brochures, floor plans, and project updates that you have explicitly requested.
  • To comply with RERA and other regulatory obligations applicable to real-estate marketing in India.
  • To improve our Site, services, and customer experience through aggregated analytics.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties except as described below:

  • Internal sales team: Your enquiry is routed to our authorised priority desk and channel partner team for follow-up.
  • RERA / government authorities: Where required by law or in response to a valid legal request.
  • Service providers: Trusted third parties who assist us in operating our Site (CRM, analytics, hosting) under strict confidentiality obligations.
